Set up my Reachys

Six short steps, about five minutes. Each one checks something on the real robots and fixes it for you. Nothing to edit by hand.

1 · Connect and find the robots

Plug both Reachy Minis into this computer with their USB cables and switch them on. The page lists what it finds and starts the software each robot needs.

2 · Which one is on which side?

Put the robots where they'll dance, facing the camera. Seen from the camera (the audience), Reachy 1 sits on the LEFT and Reachy 2 on the RIGHT.

3 · Do they move the right way?

Three quick moves. Watch both robots from the camera's side and answer. A wrong answer fixes itself, and the move plays again so you can check.

4 · Land on the beat

Motors take a moment to get somewhere, and no two robots are exactly alike. Both Reachys dance two phrases of the loaded dance while the page measures when their moves actually land, then gives each one the head start it needs. Stand back and let them dance.

5 · Match the head cameras

Each Reachy has a camera in its head, and both look the same to the browser. Reachy 1 will nod; the view that moves is its camera.

6 · Frame yourself

Choose the camera that will film you (usually the laptop's), and stand where you'll dance. Your head to your hips should fit inside the gold box with room for your arms. If you want the robots in the shot, they go at the edges of the picture.
